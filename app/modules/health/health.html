Health reminders
Notifications on this device
Drink water
A nudge to drink regularly. Skipped once you've reached your daily goal.
Get your steps in
A nudge to move. Skipped once you've reached your steps goal.
Stay active
A nudge to get your active minutes in. Skipped once you've reached your daily goal.
Sleep
A wind-down reminder before bedtime.
Reminders are created on the server at the times you set, so they are waiting in your notifications whenever you open LUMA. With device notifications on, they also reach you when LUMA is closed. Times are in your time zone.
Daily goals
The rings measure today against your goals; the + and − buttons add or take away the quick-add amount.
